# Coloring Room Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-05-13  
**Developer:** Don Noel  
**Contact:** donnoel@icloud.com

## Overview

Coloring Room is designed to be privacy-first and offline-first for normal day-to-day use. The app lets you choose built-in or imported templates, color with Apple-native tools, save your progress, and export finished artwork.

## Information the App May Handle

Coloring Room may handle the following information so the app can work:

- artwork, drawings, fills, layers, and template progress you create in the app
- imported template files you choose to add from Photos or Files
- gallery artwork metadata and related library state such as favorites, recent items, completed items, hidden items, and custom folders
- limited device or app information needed for app functionality, crash handling, or compatibility

## Local Storage and iCloud

Coloring Room is built for offline-first use. App content and state may be stored locally on your device.

If you use iCloud and have it enabled for the app, Coloring Room may sync or recover certain app data through Apple’s iCloud services, including imported templates, drawing progress, library organization, and gallery metadata, so your content can be restored across reinstalls or devices.

## Photos, Files, and Sharing

If you choose to import templates from Photos or Files, the app will access only the content you select.

If you export or share artwork, the app will create export files such as PNG images and pass them to Apple’s system share sheet when you choose to share them.

## How Information Is Used

Information handled by Coloring Room is used only to:

- provide coloring, import, save, restore, export, and gallery features
- preserve your progress and organization
- restore data when local files are unavailable and iCloud recovery is enabled
- improve reliability, stability, and usability

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share your information with third parties except:

- with Apple services you choose to use, such as iCloud or the system share sheet
- when required by law
- when necessary to protect the app, users, or legal rights

## Data Retention

Local app data is generally kept until you delete it, remove the app, or change your device or iCloud settings.

If iCloud sync is enabled, related app data may remain in your iCloud storage according to Apple’s systems and your account settings.

## Your Choices

You may be able to:

- use the app offline
- choose whether to import templates
- choose whether to use iCloud features
- delete app content from within the app where supported
- remove the app and its local data from your device

## Children’s Privacy

Coloring Room is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If material changes are made, the date above will be updated.
